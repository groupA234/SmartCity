About Us

Learn about our vision, mission, and the team behind the Smart City initiative.

Our Vision

We envision a connected, efficient, and sustainable city where every citizen has easy access to government services, real-time information, and the tools to shape their community.

The Smart City Portal was launched to bridge the gap between citizens and municipal services — replacing long queues and paperwork with seamless digital interactions available 24/7.
